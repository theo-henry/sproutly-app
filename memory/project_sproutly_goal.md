# Sproutly Goal

Sproutly is an all-in-one centralized hub for people on plant-based diets, including vegetarians and vegans.

Core areas:

- Home tab for dashboard-style discovery, quick actions, and highlights.
- Products tab for plant-based products, deals, and offers.
- Local map tab for nearby supermarkets and restaurants that are fully plant-based or have plant-based options.
- Recipes and meal planning tab with AI-assisted recipe discovery and meal plan creation.
